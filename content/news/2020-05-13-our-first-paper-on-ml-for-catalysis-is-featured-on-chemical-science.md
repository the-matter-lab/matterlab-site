As our group delves into the inverse-design of catalysts, our latest work is featured as the inside front cover of *Chemical Science*! In this [work](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2020/sc/d0sc00445f#!divAbstract), our postdocs [Pascal Friederich](https://www.int.kit.edu/1632_1492.php) and [Gabe Gomes](https://gabegomes.com) teamed up with the [Balcells group](http://www.davidbalcellsresearch.info/) at the University of Oslo to develop a workflow for the exploration of the chemical space surrounding the Vaska's complex with machine learning. 

We are excited to continue our ML-guided explorations to make better catalysts for the future!
