Our PhD student Hannah Sim is featured in the first episode of IBM Qiskit's new YouTube show "SuperPosition." Over four episodes (the other 3 will be uploaded in the near future), Hannah discusses a range of topics from how she got into quantum computing to her recent work on quantum circuit expressibility as well as ways to foster a diverse community in quantum computing.
