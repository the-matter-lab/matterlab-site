**Gabe is awarded the prestigious Banting Postdoctoral Fellowship**

Our postdoc [Gabriel dos Passos Gomes](https://gabegomes.com)  has been awarded one of Canada's [NSERC 2019-2020 Banting Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://banting.fellowships-bourses.gc.ca/en/2019-2020-eng.html) . His project is titled "*Designing Catalysts with Artificial Intelligence*", with the goal to use the synergy of artificial intelligence, robotics, and computational chemistry to accelerate the discovery of new catalysts and organic reactions. 

Congrats, Gabe!
