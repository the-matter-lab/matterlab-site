Due to the large volume of inquiries, the team can't respond to individual messages. Please **do not** contact the team directly, but rather apply through the following links.

## Postdoctoral Positions:

Apply for postdoctoral positions with The Matter Lab [here](https://zfrmz.com/KGBixh47BRxgW5cX6mln).

There are two annual calls for postdoctoral positions at the [**Vector Institute**](https://vectorinstitute.ai/) with deadlines at the end of February and at the end of August. The postdocs are independent but also work with a sponsoring research group. Apply [**here**](https://vectorinstitute.ai/programs/vector-postdoctoral-fellows/).

The [Acceleration Consortium](https://acceleration.utoronto.ca/job-board) is hiring two postdocs in the field of Robotics & Computer Vision: Apply [here](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/618ec0cd2036abb64231894a/6810e3773631c5dd046c47be_Postdoc-ChemDroid.pdf).

## Research Visitors and Internships:

The Matter Lab typically hosts undergraduate students over the summer from a variety of programs. If you are a UofT student and are interested in summer research, please fill out the [Matter Lab Undergraduate Research Application Form](https://forms.gle/b5qoVJyuMDeqv3tt9). Some of the summer programs that our students have applied in the past are listed here:  

-   IVGS: [International Visiting Graduate Students – School of Graduate Studies](https://www.sgs.utoronto.ca/future-students/international/international-visiting-graduate-students/)
-   Mitacs Programs : [Research Opportunities for Students and Postdocs](https://www.mitacs.ca/program-types/students-post-docs/)
-   Research Opportunities Program @ UofT: [Chemistry](https://www.chemistry.utoronto.ca/undergraduate/current-students/research-opportunities), [Computer Science](https://www.artsci.utoronto.ca/current/academics/research-opportunities/research-opportunities-program)
-   USRA: [NSERC - Undergraduate Student Research Awards](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/students-etudiants/ug-pc/usra-brpc_eng.asp)
-   UTEA: [Find Funding | Faculty of Arts & Science](https://www.artsci.utoronto.ca/faculty-staff/research-supports-services/find-funding#u-of-t-excellence-award-undergrad)
-   Vector internship: [https://vectorinstitute.ai/programs/internships/](https://vectorinstitute.ai/programs/internships/)
