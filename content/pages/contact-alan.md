---

-   To apply to become an undergraduate summer student, graduate student or postdoc, **do not contact Alán directly**, please visit [**Open Positions**](/basic-content-page/open-positions) for instructions on how to apply. Especially for graduate school, admissions are done by committee and he does not admit students directly.

-   If you need to talk to Alán, to discuss potential collaborations, interviews, etc. please contact [aspuru.assistant@utoronto.ca](mailto:aspuru.assistant@utoronto.ca).

-   If the topic can be discussed by email, please email Alán directly at [alan@aspuru.com](mailto:alan@aspuru.com). Due to large volumes of email response may be delayed.
